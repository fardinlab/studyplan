    FAQ

    What is Student Study Planner?

    It is a simple website where students can create and track their study plans.

    How do I add a study plan?

    Go to the Planner section, fill in the task, date, start time, end time and priority, then click Add Plan.

    Can I mark a plan as completed?

    Yes. Click the Complete button beside any study plan to mark it as completed.

    Will my plans stay after closing the browser?

    Yes. The plans are saved in the browser, so they stay on the same device and browser.
